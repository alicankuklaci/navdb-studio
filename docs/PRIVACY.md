# NavDB Studio Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 21 October 2026*

NavDB Studio is a desktop database client. It does not collect, transmit or sell personal data.

- **No accounts, analytics or telemetry.** The app sends nothing to the developer or to third parties.
- **Your connections stay on your Mac.** Connection settings, saved queries, history and preferences are stored only on your computer. Passwords are stored only when you enable *Save password*.
- **Network access** is used only to reach the database servers and URLs that you configure. In the GitHub edition, DuckDB connections may also download official DuckDB extensions from extensions.duckdb.org when a query needs one; the Mac App Store edition never downloads code.
- **Files** are read or written only when you choose them in a file dialog.
- **Removing your data.** Everything the app stores lives in its own data folder (in the Mac App Store edition, the app's sandbox container). Deleting a connection removes it, and deleting the app removes all of it.
- **Purchases** in the Mac App Store edition are handled entirely by Apple. The app only receives whether a subscription is active; it never sees your payment details. See [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/alicankuklaci/navdb-studio/issues>.
