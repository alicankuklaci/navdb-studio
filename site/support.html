Frequently asked questions

How do I try it without a server?

Choose SQLite or DuckDB on the welcome screen and create a new database file, or leave the DuckDB path empty for an in-memory database.

Restoring NavDB Studio Pro

Open Help > NavDB Studio Pro… and click Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you subscribed with.

Cancelling a subscription

Open the App Store, click your name, then Account Settings > Subscriptions > Manage, or use Manage subscription in the Pro window.

Opening files outside the sandbox

The app can only use files and folders you choose in an Open or Save dialog; it remembers that permission for next time.
