Privacy Policy

NavDB Studio

OverviewSupportPrivacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

NavDB Studio is a desktop database client. It does not collect, transmit or sell personal data.

  • No accounts, analytics or telemetry. The app sends nothing to the developer or to third parties.
  • Your connections stay on your Mac. Connection settings, saved queries, history and preferences are stored only on your computer. Passwords are stored only when you enable Save password.
  • Network access is used only to reach the database servers and URLs that you configure. The app never downloads executable code.
  • Files are read or written only when you choose them in a file dialog.
  • Removing your data. Everything the app stores lives in its sandbox container. Deleting a connection removes it, and deleting the app removes all of it.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. The app only learns whether a subscription is active; it never sees your payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.
